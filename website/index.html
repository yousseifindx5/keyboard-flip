How it works

Three seconds, no copy-paste.

Select the text

Highlight what you typed in the wrong language. Nothing selected? It converts the last word.

Press your shortcut

Every conversion has its own shortcut. For example Ctrl Alt L for English → Arabic, you can change it later.

It’s fixed in place

The text is replaced right where it was, and your clipboard is put back as it was. Not right? Press the undo shortcut.

Fast

One shortcut cycles the selected text through ALL CAPS, Sentence case, and all lowercase.

Snippets

A shortcut opens a small list of the things you save, like your email or phone number. Pick one and it is pasted. Stored only on your PC.

Preview before replacing

Optional. See the result first, then confirm. Off by default so the fix stays one keystroke.

👆

Optional. Tap the key twice instead of pressing a longer shortcut. Off by default.

History

See your latest conversions and copy from them. It is saved on your PC only while the program runs, deleted when you quit it completely, and you can turn it off or clear it.

English, Arabic, German, French, Spanish and Russian.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. It’s free and open source. No ads, no account, no paid version.

Is this a translator?

No. It doesn’t change the meaning. It re-types the same key presses on another keyboard layout, which is exactly what you need after typing with the wrong language on.

Does it need internet or collect my data?

No telemetry. It works offline; the only optional internet use is an update check you can turn on (off by default), and it only asks GitHub for the latest version number. Your text never leaves your computer, and it only reads your selection when you press the shortcut.

Where are my snippets and history kept?

Snippets are stored on your PC only. The conversion history is saved on your PC only while the program runs, and is deleted when you quit the app completely. You can also turn it off or clear it any time.

Does double-tap Shift or Ctrl record what I type?

No. It is off by default. When you turn it on, the app only watches the timing of Shift or Ctrl presses to notice a double tap. Other keys are never read, saved or sent anywhere. Some antivirus programs warn about any app that watches the keyboard, and since the code is open you can check this yourself.

Does it mess with my clipboard?

It briefly uses the clipboard to copy and paste, then restores your previous text. Images or files you had copied are not restored.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. For now it’s Windows 10 and 11 only.

It doesn’t work inside one program. Why?

Programs running as Administrator block other apps from sending keys to them. Run Keyboard Flip as Administrator too, or check that the program isn’t in your excluded list.
