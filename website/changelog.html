Changelog

What’s new in each version, newest first.

1.1.0

Latest5 November 2026

New

  • 8 new languages: Turkish, Ukrainian, Persian, Hindi, Urdu, Italian, Portuguese and Greek. They are off by default; turn them on in the Keyboards tab.
  • Change letter case: a new shortcut, Ctrl Alt U, cycles the selected text through ALL CAPS, Sentence case and all lowercase.
  • Snippets menu: a shortcut opens a small list of your saved snippets, such as your email or phone number. Pick one and it is pasted. Snippets are stored only on your PC.
  • Preview before replacing: optional, off by default.
  • Double-tap Shift or Ctrl: an optional shortcut. Off by default.
  • Undo several times: you can now undo up to 10 conversions in a row.
  • History: see and copy from your latest conversions. It is saved on your PC only while the program runs and deleted when you quit it completely, and can be turned off or cleared.
  • Welcome tour: a short first-run tour with a practice box.
  • Look and feel: accent colors, new Midnight and Sand themes.
  • New logo and a sharper icon in the taskbar.
  • Right-to-left Arabic: with the Arabic interface, the whole window is mirrored.
  • Skip apps: choose a program to skip with a normal Browse window.
  • Interface in 6 languages: English, Arabic, German, French, Spanish and Russian.

Privacy and releases

  • Optional update check: off by default. When you turn it on, it only asks GitHub for the latest version number.
  • Builds on GitHub: releases can now be built on GitHub, which prepares the project for free code signing from SignPath.

1.0.1

Bug fixes
  • Fixed capital D, F, C and V (typed with Shift) being converted wrongly when switching between English and Arabic.

1.0.0

First public release
  • 6 languages: Arabic, English, German, French, Spanish and Russian.
  • Up to 10 shortcuts, one for each direction, plus a “both ways” option.
  • Undo, and convert the last word when nothing is selected.
  • Excluded apps, a system tray icon and optional start with Windows.
  • Light and dark themes, with an English and Arabic interface.
  • Fully offline, no telemetry.
